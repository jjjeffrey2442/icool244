# OTG Fitness Privacy Policy

*Effective October 17, 2026*

OTG Fitness is a workout companion app made by OTG Factory. The short version: **we collect no data about you.** Everything you enter stays on your phone.

## What the app stores
The app saves the workouts, sets, weights, plans, recovery sessions and settings you enter. This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

## What we collect
Nothing. OTG Fitness has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We cannot see your data, and we do not sell or share it, because we never have it.

## Third parties
The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Apple may collect crash reports and usage statistics if you have agreed to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings; that is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deleting your data
Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

## Children
The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
